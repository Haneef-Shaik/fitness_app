The rules every screen is held to

RuleWhy
A set commit never awaits the networkp95 tap-to-rendered under 100 ms. Offline is indistinguishable from online except for the sync dot.
Estimated never looks like confirmedDashed border, sparkle, “Est.” chip — and it adds nothing to any total until you confirm it.
A regression is never redA lighter training day is information, not an error. Critical is reserved for what you must fix.
Filtered-empty ≠ genuinely emptyTelling someone with 84 sessions “no workouts yet” because a filter excluded them is a bug.
Colour never carries meaning aloneEvery status pairs a hue with an icon, a label, or both.
The PR celebration waits for the summaryInterrupting someone mid-set to congratulate them is the worst thing the logger could do.
Any AI failure leaves training untouchedWorkout logging, manual food entry, history and analytics never depend on the AI service.
