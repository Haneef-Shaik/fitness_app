**Low 3: the trailing-space note cites phase 3J's rows, but these are different rows.**

Across the parts I compared line by line (raw 1–120, 690–749 and 860–889, which include all the kept lines), they were the only differences besides the two cuts. No further byte count is needed.
- **Fix:** record "7 trailing spaces removed, at raw lines 96, 98, 100, 101, 103, 104 and 107, by this review's Grep".
