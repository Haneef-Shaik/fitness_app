# R3 evidence base

These six briefs are the research behind
[R3 — Gyms as the distribution channel](../R3-gym-b2b2c-strategy.md). Each was compiled on
**2 Oct 2026** by a separate web-research agent working from one brief, and each is kept as it was
written, so that any number in the report can be traced back to its source, URL and date.

| Brief | Covers | Words |
|---|---|---:|
| [A — Indian gym software](A-india-gym-software.md) | ~40 Indian gym-software products (Play Store installs, prices, owner reviews), how independent gyms actually run, attendance hardware prices, Deloitte–HFA market numbers, GST | ~3,700 |
| [B — Indian consumer fitness](B-india-consumer-fitness.md) | Cult.fit (DRHP), FITPASS, Fitternity, Healthify, Fittr, Fitelo, Ultrahuman, Hudle/Playo; Indian willingness to pay; complaints | ~4,600 |
| [C — Global gym software](C-global-gym-software.md) | Playlist (Mindbody + ClassPass + EGYM), ABC, PushPress (free plan), Zen Planner, Wodify, TeamUp, GymMaster, ClubRight, Keepme, Hevy Coach; member-app adoption; retention science | ~4,600 |
| [D — Consumer apps and virality](D-consumer-apps-and-virality.md) | Hevy, Strong, Fitbod, Boostcamp, Ladder, Strava, MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, MacroFactor; AI photo-calorie accuracy studies; retention benchmarks; social and gamification research; portability | ~4,500 |
| [E — B2B2C analog cases](E-b2b2c-analogs.md) | Fresha, Booksy, Treatwell, Mindbody/ClassPass, Playtomic, Practo, Doctolib, Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar, Petpooja, Toast, Shopify, OpenTable, Zomato Gold; network-effect theory and its critiques | ~4,600 |

## How far to trust them

- Every claim carries the agent's tag: **[FACT]**, **[COMPANY CLAIM]**, **[USER FEEDBACK]**,
  **[ESTIMATE]**, **[UNVERIFIED]** or **[ANALYSIS]** (F also uses **[VENDOR PRICE]** and
  **[LEGAL INTERPRETATION]**). The report keeps that distinction in its own labels.
- The agents could not reach Reddit, the Economic Times or Capterra's full pages, and a shared
  search budget ran out late in the run. Gaps are marked "not found" rather than filled.
- Nothing here was checked by a lawyer. Brief F is **not legal advice**.
- One fact moved after its brief was written: NPCI's UPI merchant fee (see the correction note at
  the top of F).
