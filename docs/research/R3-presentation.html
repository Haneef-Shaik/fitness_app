The idea we tested

Give gym software away free, and let each gym bring its members to FitLog

1

! Plausible

✓ Our strength

No gym app has a real logger or Indian nutrition. FitLog does.

Engagement → gym value

✓ Evidence supports

First-month visits, staff contact and friends predict who stays.

Gym pushes harder

! Needs proof

Only if the owner sees more renewals, in rupees.

6

Network across gyms

✕ Weak

The answer

Yes, as the gym's membership card, not as a network across gyms

✓ What works

The app is how members use their gym

Fees and receipts, the trainer's plan, and self check-in that counts for challenges. It is not an entry gate. FitLog's logger and nutrition keep them.

✕ What's weak

Members carried between gyms

Marketplaces stay thin even for Mindbody and Fresha, and they turn owners against the platform. Phase 3 at the earliest.

→ What decides it

Member activation through the gym

One number, and cheap to measure: a 5-gym, 10-week pilot.

The network effect the evidence supports is inside each gym: owner, trainers and members on one system. It works from the first gym.

The gap we'd close

One gap, seen from two sides

The gym's software doesn't know what members do

  • No early warning of who is drifting
  • Plans and diet charts on paper and WhatsApp
  • Renewals sent as bare invoices
  • Personal-training sessions counted by hand
A shared system
Trainer assigns → member logs → owner sees → renewal shows progress

The member's app doesn't know the gym exists

  • No plan from their trainer
  • Nobody notices when they skip
  • No competition with the people they actually train with
  • Gone when the membership ends
Neither gym software nor a consumer app alone can close this gap. FitLog already has the consumer half.

Retention · owner side

Absence alerts are free. AI calling is Pro, priced by usage.

✓ Free

Alert, then one-tap WhatsApp

No visit in 7 days · new member under 4 visits in month one · renewal due while attendance drops. A daily push to the owner and a ranked list. Re-engagement contact cut cancellations by 45% in a UK study.

→ Pro

The AI calls on the gym's behalf

In Hindi, Hinglish or English. It says up front that it is automated, checks in warmly, offers a trainer slot or a freeze, and logs the reason in the owner's list.

Tier (illustrative)Per month

Extra minutes₹15/min

Voice AI costs ~₹6–9 a minute in production. Prices are before GST and keep ≥1.5–2× margin.

Data rule: without an entry gate, attendance is partial, so alerts are flagged "confident" only where visits are reliably captured. Compliance: automated calls must declare themselves (TRAI, Sept 2026); counsel review before launch.

Trust is the edge

Five written promises to gym owners

  1. We never show another gym to your active members.
  2. No commission on members you bring, including through your own QR codes.
  3. Your data is yours. Free export, any time.
  4. Your members see your gym's name in the app.
  5. If a member leaves, we tell you and help you win them back, and show them no other gym for at least 30 days. We recommend making that permanent.

Why it matters

  • Cult.fit already offers independents software and demand, but it competes with them
  • Booksy charged salons commission on clients who "scan our QR code"
  • ClassPass and Zomato Gold triggered walk-outs
Report §6.7 and §12; evidence briefs A, B, E.
Go-to-market · India

One dense city cluster, gym by gym, in person

Beachhead: independent, owner-run gyms with 150–500 members paying ₹700–1,500 a month, 2+ trainers, on a register, Excel or a cheap app, in a city the team can visit weekly.

10 gyms

Founder-led. Opens with a free "dues and drop-off audit" of the owner's register.

1,000 users

10 gyms × ~250 × 40%. 8–12 weeks after go-live.

10,000 users

~90 gyms, 2–3 field reps, trainer ambassadors, first inter-gym challenge. 6–9 months after the pilot.

100,000 users

~1,000 gyms in 3–5 cities; biometric-device dealers and payment partners. 18–30 months.

White-glove for the first ~100 gyms. Self-serve only once it matches white-glove activation. Installs are not activation.

The decision

Approve the validation, not the build

WEEKS 0–3

Learn

  • 20–30 owners and 30–50 members interviewed
  • Counsel reviews the consent design
  • AI accuracy test on weighed Indian meals
  • Hand-run version with 2 gyms
WEEKS 3–13

Pilot

  • 5 independent gyms, one city
  • Gym MVP parts switched on as they ship
  • ≈ ₹40–60k cash, plus founder time
  • Consumer store launch continues in parallel
WEEK 13

Decide

  • ✓ Scale when all hold:≥ 40% of active members linked by day 60
    ≥ 25% of them log every week
    Renewals up in ≥ 3 of 5 gyms
    ≥ 2 of 5 owners say yes to Pro
  • ! Fix trending up, short of that
  • ✕ Pivot under 15% linked
