The short version

  • FitLog keeps what you log — workouts, meals, body measurements, goals and photos — so it can show you how they change. That is the only thing we use it for.
  • We do not sell your data, show you ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • Food photos and meal descriptions are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, only when you ask for an analysis. The result is an estimate that you review before anything is saved.
  • You can download everything, delete your photos, or delete your whole account at any time — in the app, or on the web at {{delete_url}}. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • FitLog is for people aged 16 and over. It is not a medical service.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and the date you joined.
  • Your password — stored only as a one-way hash (Argon2). We never see or store the password itself.
  • Sign-in sessions on your devices, stored as hashes so a leaked database cannot be used to sign in as you.

Your profile

  • Display name, birth date, sex and height, activity level, training experience, days per week and session length you train, the equipment you have, your units, time zone and week start, your calorie and macro targets (and the history of past targets), and how often you want check-ins.
  • We use your birth date, sex, height and activity level to estimate an energy target when you ask for one, and your birth date to confirm you are 16 or over.

What you log

  • Workouts: programs, planned days, exercises (including any you create), workout sessions, sets (weight, repetitions, effort ratings), notes and personal records.
  • Nutrition: meals, the foods and amounts in them, foods and recipes you create, and your meal categories.
  • Body metrics: body weight, measurements and any notes you add.
  • Progress photos you take or choose in the app.
  • Goals you set and how they progress.

Food analysis

  • Food photos and meal descriptions you submit for analysis, and the analysis itself: the foods the model identified, its estimated amounts, calories and macros, its confidence, the model's name and version, and whether you saved, changed or discarded the result. We keep that record so you can always see what the AI claimed and what you actually logged.

Technical data

  • The IP address a request comes from, used to limit repeated sign-in and deletion attempts and AI requests (see Security). Counters are keyed by address and expire within 24 hours.
  • Server logs of requests: the time, the route, the response code and a request ID — the reference you can quote to support. Our logging rules exclude email addresses, what you typed, photos and tokens. [OWNER: confirm log retention, e.g. 30 days.]
  • Crash and error reports from the app, if crash reporting is enabled: device model, operating system, app version and the error. [OWNER: name the crash-reporting provider, or delete this item if none is used.]

What we do not collect

  • Location. The app removes location and camera metadata (EXIF) from photos before upload, and our server removes it again on arrival.
  • Your contacts, advertising identifiers, or data from other apps. FitLog does not connect to Apple Health, Google Fit or wearables at this time.

We process it only because you choose to enter it in order to use FitLog, and on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give when you create an account and enter it. 3. How we use it

  • To provide FitLog: store what you log, sync it between your devices, and calculate your history, charts, personal records and targets.
  • To analyse food photos and descriptions when you ask (section 4).
  • To keep the service secure: authenticate you, detect reuse of stolen sessions, and limit repeated attempts.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. AI food analysis

When — and only when — you tap to analyse a meal photo or a written description, that photo or text is sent to Anthropic PBC, which provides the Claude model that identifies the foods and estimates their amounts. We send the photo or text and nothing else: not your name, email, or other logs. The result is shown to you for review; nothing is saved to your diary until you confirm it, and you can change or discard anything.

Anthropic processes this content on our behalf as a service provider. 8. Age

FitLog is for people aged 16 and over. The app will not accept a birth date under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not medical advice

FitLog is a tracking tool, not a medical device or a health service. Its figures — AI food estimates, calorie and macro targets, estimated one-rep maxes — are estimates for your information, not medical, dietary or clinical advice.
